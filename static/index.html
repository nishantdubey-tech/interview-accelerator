How Forma Works

1. Structured Role & Candidate Extraction

Your JD and resume are parsed for required competencies, achievements, and claims requiring follow-up questioning.

2. Transparent Job Fit Engine

Scores are calculated using a 6-factor evidence model: Required Skills (30%), Technical (25%), Experience (15%), Projects (15%), Behavioural (10%), Qualifications (5%).

3. 3-Level Adaptive Interview Simulator

• Level 1 (Screening): Evaluates motivation, ownership, and resume claims.
• Level 2 (Competency): Tests technical depth, problem-solving, and architecture.
• Level 3 (Deep-Dive): Challenges assumptions, probes metrics, and asks why/how.

4. Voice & Cloud Fallback

Speak using Chrome/Edge Web Speech API, or use Cloud Audio Fallback on Safari/Firefox, or type directly.
