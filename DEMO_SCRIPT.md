# Demo Video Script — Forma Interview Accelerator
**Target Duration:** 3 to 5 minutes  
**Recommended Browser:** Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (for full Web Speech API voice support)

---

### Step-by-Step Recording Guide

#### 0:00 – 0:30 | Introduction & Landing Dashboard
- **Screen:** Forma Landing Page (`/`)
- **Action:**
  - Show the sleek emerald dark theme, top navigation, and system status indicator (*"Gemini Live AI (Active)"*).
  - Highlight the 1-click **Quick-Load Presets** (*"AI / RAG Engineer"*, *"Full-Stack Engineer"*, *"AI Product Manager"*).
  - Click **AI / RAG Engineer** to populate realistic, high-signal JD and Resume data instantly.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Welcome to Forma — an AI-powered Interview Accelerator designed to bridge the gap between static practice questions and authentic high-stakes technical interviews. Unlike generic interview prep, Forma reads the specific nuances of your target job description and your actual resume to build an adaptive, 3-level simulation."*

---

#### 0:30 – 1:15 | Profile Analysis & Transparent Job Fit Engine
- **Screen:** Analysis & Job Fit View
- **Action:**
  - Click **"Analyze My Profile"**.
  - Review the **Job Fit Score** (e.g. 84%) and walk through the **6 weighted dimensions** (Required Skills 30%, Technical Competencies 25%, Experience 15%, Projects 15%, Behavioural 10%, Qualifications 5%).
  - Highlight the **Strong / Partial / Weak** category badges and concrete evidence/gap notes.
  - Switch tabs to **Role Analysis** (responsibilities, required & preferred skills, concepts) and **Candidate Evidence** (strengths, weak areas, and specifically the *Resume Claims to Probe*).
- **Narration:**
  > *"Forma doesn't ask the LLM for a hallucinated percentage. Instead, the server mathematically computes a defensible Job Fit score from 6 weighted evidence dimensions. We can also inspect the exact resume claims the AI has flagged to probe during the interview."*

---

#### 1:15 – 2:30 | AI Interview Simulator (Voice AI & Real-Time Adaptation)
- **Screen:** AI Interview Simulator Room
- **Action:**
  - Click **"Start AI Interview Simulator"**.
  - Show the animated AI avatar orb. Listen to the browser Text-to-Speech (TTS) speaking the personalized opening question.
  - Point out that the question references the candidate's actual projects (*"I noticed that you architected an enterprise RAG pipeline serving 40k queries..."*).
  - Toggle the **Candidate Webcam Preview** on to demonstrate the video interview feature.
  - Click **"Start Speaking"** (or use the Cloud Audio Fallback). Speak an answer referencing the 28% latency improvement and hybrid BM25 + dense reranking.
  - Note the real-time **Speech Analytics** (duration timer, WPM pace, and filler word counter).
  - Click **"Submit Answer"**.
  - Show the **Instant Micro-Evaluation Card** (Answer Score, strengths, and the transparent rationale explaining how the next question was selected).
- **Narration:**
  > *"Notice how the interviewer doesn't ask generic icebreakers. It immediately references our actual project evidence. I can speak using real-time voice transcription or fall back to cloud audio recording and text typing. After submitting, the AI evaluates my answer in real time and tells me exactly why it is selecting the next follow-up."*

---

#### 2:30 – 3:30 | 3-Stage Progression & Deep-Dive Probing
- **Action:**
  - Answer the next question with a specific technical rationale. Show how the follow-up question causally adapts to the details just spoken (*"You mentioned using BM25 with dense vector reranking. How did you handle index synchronization and what evaluation metric proved the 28% reduction?"*).
  - Submit answers to showcase stage progression:
    - **Level 1 (Screening):** Resume claims and project ownership.
    - **Level 2 (Competency):** Distributed systems design and API trade-offs.
    - **Level 3 (Deep-Dive):** Failure recovery, edge cases, and challenging assumptions.
- **Narration:**
  > *"This is the core differentiator: the interview adapts dynamically. If I give a strong answer, it increases technical depth. If I'm vague, it probes for metrics. As we advance from Screening to Competency to Deep-Dive, the difficulty and rigor adapt in real time."*

---

#### 3:30 – 4:30 | Performance Report & Prioritized Preparation Gaps
- **Screen:** Final Performance & Preparation Report
- **Action:**
  - Click **"Finish & See Report"**.
  - Walk through the summary cards:
    - **Overall Interview Score:** (e.g. 82/100)
    - **Job Fit Score:** (e.g. 84%)
    - **Interview Readiness Score:** (e.g. 83/100 — 🟢 *Strong Candidate* / 🟡 *Interview Ready*)
  - Show the **7 Core Competency Breakdown** progress bars (Role Fit, Technical Knowledge, Problem Solving, Communication, Confidence, Depth, Behavioural).
  - Review the **Demonstrated Strengths** and **Growth Areas** grounded in actual answers.
  - Highlight the **Prioritized Preparation Gap Engine** (Priority 1, 2 with exact review topics and mock practice questions).
  - Show the **Question-by-Question Deep Dive** (What Was Good, What Could Be Better, Ideal Direction).
  - Click **"Print / Save PDF"** to demonstrate the publication-grade print stylesheet.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Finally, Forma synthesizes an evidence-based preparation plan. The readiness score is a logical composite of 70% interview performance and 30% job fit. Candidates leave with prioritized study topics and specific mock questions to master before their real interview."*
