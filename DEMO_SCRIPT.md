# Demo video script (about 4 minutes)

Use a real job description and an anonymized resume. Configure a valid provider key before recording. Use Chrome or Edge and allow microphone access. Do not show API keys in the recording.

**0:00–0:20 — Set the scene**  
“Forma turns a role brief and your own experience into focused interview practice, then builds a plan from the answers you actually gave.” Show the preparation page and the three-step path.

**0:20–0:50 — Add the inputs**  
Paste or upload the real JD and resume. Point out PDF/DOCX/TXT support and the private session note. Click **Build my preparation**.

**0:50–1:25 — Review the analysis and fit**  
Show the extracted role title, role skills, candidate strengths, preparation areas, weighted Job Fit, and each dimension's evidence and gaps. Explain that the percentage is calculated from six weighted dimensions rather than requested as a model guess.

**1:25–2:00 — Screening and actual voice**  
Start the interview. Read the personalized first question. Press replay to hear browser speech synthesis. Press the microphone and answer aloud; show the live/final transcript in the editable answer box, then submit. Point out the answer score and feedback.

**2:00–2:40 — Prove adaptation**  
Give a specific answer with a measurable claim, such as a project result. Read the generated next question aloud and show how it probes the detail just spoken. Submit a contrasting vague or incomplete answer next and show that the interviewer clarifies or scaffolds. The question is generated after each answer, with the transcript and previous feedback in its context.

**2:40–3:15 — Three levels**  
Continue through answer three and answer six. Show progression from Screening to Competency to Deep-dive, and emphasize that each new question still responds to the answer rather than drawing from a static list.

**3:15–3:50 — Report and next steps**  
Choose **Finish & see my report**. Show overall performance, job fit, readiness, competency scores, strengths, improvement areas, question-level feedback, and preparation gaps. Mention that readiness combines interview performance and role fit and is a coaching estimate.

**3:50–4:00 — Close**  
“The point is to practice your evidence, identify the gaps, and leave with a concrete next step.” Optionally show print/save report.

Recording checklist: real inputs; no placeholder examples; provider configured; mic permission granted; demonstrate actual spoken transcript; show a follow-up that refers to the immediately prior answer; show all three levels; show report; hide secrets and personal contact details.
